Size trades. Manage risk.

Tickwise is a futures trading calculator for iPhone. Work out profit, loss, risk/reward and position size before you place a trade.

  • 27 popular CME futures contracts, including E-mini and Micro
  • Exact take-profit prices for any risk-to-reward ratio, with 1R to 5R levels
  • Position sizing from your account size and risk percentage
  • Trade journal with realized P&L and PDF export
  • Market sessions, optional open alerts, and news with an impact rating
  • Widgets and Shortcuts

Support

Questions, bugs or feature requests: please open an issue. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you expected to happen.

Important

Tickwise is a calculator. It does not place trades, connect to a broker or give financial advice. Futures trading carries a high risk of loss. Contract specifications and margins are estimates and vary by broker. News impact ratings are automated estimates of importance, not predictions of price direction. Tickwise is not affiliated with CME Group, any exchange, or any news publisher.
