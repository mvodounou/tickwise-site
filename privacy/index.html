Tickwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Tickwise (“the app”) is a futures trading calculator for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Tickwise has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own. We do not collect, store or receive any personal information, trades or usage data.

What stays on your device

  • Your journal entries, notes and settings are stored on your device.
  • Optional market alerts are scheduled on your device as local notifications.

Third-party services you connect to

To show news, the app downloads public RSS feeds directly from the Federal Reserve, the European Central Bank and the U.S. Energy Information Administration, and, if you turn it on, CNBC. To show prices, the app requests the latest futures price from Massive (massive.com). These providers can see your IP address, as any website does, and handle it under their own privacy policies. We do not receive that information.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. When you export a trade as a PDF, the file goes only where you choose to send it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue.

Not financial advice

Tickwise provides calculations for education and record keeping. It does not give financial advice. Futures trading carries a high risk of loss.
